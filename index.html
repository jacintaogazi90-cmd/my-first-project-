Our Heritage

A century of
Swiss precision

Founded in Geneva in 1924, AURUM has spent a century perfecting the art of fine watchmaking. Every component — from the mainspring to the sapphire crystal — is selected and finished to standards that exceed industry norms. Our atelier employs fewer than forty master watchmakers, each with decades of experience.
